Snack

A physical timekeeper that sits on the back of your phone. One click starts a Snack Break and blocks the apps you chose; a second click ends it, and the app keeps an honest record of where your time went.

Work
Mechanical design, packaging, DFM package
When
2026
Tools
Fusion 360, scripted fit audit
Size
64 × 78 × 7.6 mm

What I did

  • Restarted the design at revision R03, cutting the stack from 9.8 mm to 7.6 mm, with 7.8 mm as a hard limit.
  • Fit two boards, a flex, a battery, a MagSafe-aligned ring and a dead-front glass into the frame, each in a named volume.
  • Wrote a scripted audit of 83 critical fits, down to 0.15 mm between the USB-C connector and its cage.
  • Placed the ring on the MagSafe axis from Apple’s accessory drawing, clear of the camera plateau and the zone Apple asks accessories to leave free.
  • Packaged revision R07 for a manufacturer’s DFM review: a BOM, a source ledger, an interface net table and a release checklist.

Under 8 mm

A clean start

Revision R03 threw out the 9.8 mm stack and started clean at 64 by 78 by 7.6 mm. Every part had to fit a named volume after walls, corner radii, adhesive and battery swell, with 7.8 mm as a hard limit.

Two boards in 7.6 mm

A main board with the USB-C port, a display board joined to it by a flex, the cell, the attachment ring and a dead-front glass, stacked inside the frame. A scripted audit checks 83 critical fits, down to 0.15 mm between the USB-C connector and its cage.

Lights up through the glass

The face is dead front: 512 warm-white LEDs in a 32 by 16 grid sit behind the glass, invisible until they light. They confirm the snap, then run a break timer.

Made for the back of an iPhone

On the MagSafe axis

The attachment ring sits on the MagSafe axis from Apple’s accessory drawing, so Snack lands in the same place on every iPhone 17 Pro. Its top edge stops about 4 mm short of the camera plateau, and the top 10 mm is a nonconductive cap that keeps the metal frame out of the zone Apple asks accessories to leave clear.

Ready for a manufacturer’s review

R07 is packaged for an OEM design-for-manufacture review: a BOM, a source ledger for every part, an interface net table, and a release checklist where nothing closes without an owner, an artifact, a date and an approver.
