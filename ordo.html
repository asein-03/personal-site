Ordo

A calendar that plans your day for you. Ordo reads your calendars, knows your classes and what’s due, and places your tasks into the time you actually have. While you focus, it pauses the apps that pull you away.

Download on the App Store useordoapp.com

What I did

  • Designed the app end to end: what it plans, how it shows a day, and what it leaves out. Nothing keeps score: no points, no streaks, no percentages.
  • Built it with AI coding tools and launched it on the App Store on August 12, 2026.
  • Shipped updates almost every week since; version 2.0.1 went out on October 30.
  • Connected it to Google and Outlook calendars, Apple Health, WHOOP and Apple Maps.
  • Designed and printed Ordo Desktop, a desk display for it.
